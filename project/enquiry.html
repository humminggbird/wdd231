Why solar, and what it really costs

Ghana gets strong sunshine all year, and grid power is not always steady. Solar answers both problems, but the sticker price of a panel is only part of the story. This page explains the rest, and the form at the bottom lets you ask for a recommendation.

Three things worth knowing

Cost per watt beats price

A cheaper panel is not cheaper if it makes less power. Dividing the price by the wattage gives the cost per watt, and that single number lets you compare a 425 W panel against a 665 W one fairly.

Importing is not free

A panel that looks cheap on a Chinese supplier site still has to reach Tema, clear customs and get to your roof. Shipping, duty and clearing charges can add a large share of the base price.

Efficiency matters on small roofs

Efficiency is how much of the sunlight hitting the panel becomes electricity. On a small roof a more efficient panel earns its extra cost, because you simply cannot fit more panels.
